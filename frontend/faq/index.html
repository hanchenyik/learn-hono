Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our curated pieces, ordering process, and authenticity verification system.

Are all listed pieces available? ↓

Availability is shown on each product page. An out-of-stock piece cannot be added to your cart.

Is this a real payment flow? ↓

No. The QR and card screens simulate payment. No real money is charged, and card details stay in your browser.

What does Catalog Lookup check? ↓

Enter a catalog ID to find a listed product. This lookup does not verify the authenticity of a physical item.

Where does the application data live? ↓

Your account and order details are stored by the service. Your cart stays in this browser until you complete checkout or remove its items.

How can I contact client services? ↓

Visit the Contact page for boutique details or prepare an inquiry email there.
